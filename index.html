In person event: First Membership Consultation on FSC PCI: Yaoundé, Cameroon

📆 Date: June 4–5, 2025

🎯 Audience: FSC members (by invitation)

  • FSC hosted its first in-person consultation for the PCI revision process in Yaoundé, Cameroon, with over 25 members attending.
  • The event launched a new phase in revising FSC's core standards, with strong participation from African members and staff.
  • Members shared views on key issues such as Indigenous Peoples’ rights, gender equity, worker protections, climate change, and certification benefits.
  • A strong call was made for making the PCI more relevant to African contexts and ensuring broader engagement across the region.
